# Khandoker Tanjim Ahammad

**Doctoral Researcher in Agricultural Engineering** at the **University of Hohenheim**

I develop reproducible imaging and automation methods for agricultural research. My work combines drone-based thermal and RGB sensing, depth and hyperspectral imaging, computer vision, deep learning, robotics, and modular phenotyping platforms.

## Current research focus

- UAV thermal and RGB imaging for crop monitoring and field phenotyping
- Depth-sensor 3D phenotyping of maize morphology
- Computer vision and deep learning for plant-trait extraction
- Hyperspectral imaging for non-destructive plant diagnostics
- Robotics, sensor integration, and phenotyping platforms
- Particle-size-aware modelling for Agri-PV soiling and maintenance

## Selected posters and contributions

- **Benchmarking Depth-Sensor 3D Phenotyping of Maize Cobs Against 2D Image and Geometric Methods** - CIGR EurAgEng World Congress 2026
- **Semi-empirical prediction of dust soiling effects on Agri-PV output power** - 2026 research poster
- **Detecting phosphorus deficiency in maize plant leaves using hyperspectral imaging** - Tropentag 2025
- **Automated morphometric quantification of maize cob kernels via lightweight RGB image analysis and Cellpose-based deep learning segmentation** - Tropentag 2026 contribution
